# M3 attempt 1: why guided lost, and what v2 changes (2026-09-29)

**Verdict.** Mostly noise, plus two harms caused by the system message itself. The whole pass gap sits in overflow runs: without an exception both arms pass alike (control 32/44, guided 31/44), but control had a passing deliverable on disk when the window ran out in 9 of 18 overflows against guided's 6 of 19. Guided overflows die sooner (median 9 vs 11.5 turns) and more often contain a reply with over 40k characters of reasoning (12/19 vs 7/18).
- **Harm 1, first-reply format breaks.** The first sample failed Terminus-2's parser on turn 1 in 14 of 65 guided episodes against 1 of 64 in control: `<tool_call>` markup, a fake `root@...:/app#` prompt, `</think>` inside the content. 6 unparseable replies reached harbor. Autofixed tool calls lost the trailing newline, so the command was typed but never ran. In debugging_090344_004 (guided ep49), turns 1-5 were lost this way. At turn 10 the reply threw away its own 29k-character plan and began again with "I'll start by exploring"; the run overflowed. The v1 format paragraph names "tool-call tags", which probably primes them.
- **Harm 2, the task is read as missing.** In 7 guided episodes against 1 in control, the reasoning concluded there was no task. Qwen took Terminus-2's "You are an AI assistant..." message for "the system prompt explaining the JSON format" and the v1 persona for the real prompt. In system-administration_093408_002 (turn 3) it wrote "I only received the system prompt, no explicit task description". It then spent turns 3-7 searching `/` for a task file and missed the IPv6 `localhost` check that control caught from `/etc/hosts`.
- **Lines with no effect.** "Stop exploring once the conversation has grown long" is never cited. "Think in proportion" and "never work out by hand" are ignored. security_051646_001 derived the SHA-512 constants by hand in a 47k-character turn and got them wrong. software-engineering_144210_022 thought for 66k characters before its first `ls`.
- **"Rely only on the clean grader environment."** Cited in 13 guided runs vs 3, and treated as task text ("The task note says...", 045800_002 turn 7). Installs did not change (8 vs 7). In security_174918_002, guided hand-wrote an x86 disassembler and overflowed; control ran `apt-get install binutils` and passed. That is one case, not a trend.
- **Noise and weak checks.** Three losses are spec luck: sqli 59 vs 56 under "or similar indicators", a hidden RPATH check, and a both-arm overflow. In optimization_204204_001, guided checked at 2,000 iterations where the benchmark uses 500, against its own copy of the same algorithm. That check could not fail. In 143956_003, guided made no edit in 9 turns; it spent them on a reference validator and a stale `.o` before overflowing. At least two of the 5 guided-only wins came from comparing against an independent computation: a pure-Python reference in data-science_112147_008, and a re-parse of the raw sources in data-processing_210354_002.

**Uptake.** The system message reached all 795 guided requests (checked in the bodies). Qwen followed rules that name a concrete action: the repro script, the cross-check, clean-env thoughts. It cited them about 20 times ("per the instructions", "the system prompt says"), which is a leak risk. It ignored the budget and format rules. v1 also aimed at the wrong prefix. 85-88 % of pre-JSON text states a result ("All checks passed with 2000 iterations..."), not the "I'll start by..." announcement v1 targeted. Guided produced more of it (195 vs 115 turns), and the prefix repeats on 67-81 % of following turns.

**Changes in v2.**
- No persona. The first sentence says where the task is.
- Format stated in positive terms, with no tool-call wording; findings go in `analysis`.
- "Settle by command", with concrete examples, and "install rather than rebuild".
- A first complete deliverable early. The "stop exploring when long" line is dropped.
- Check "exactly as the task specifies, never an easier version" and keep the independent comparison.
- The repro rule cut to one sentence.
- The three habits repeated in a 40-word reminder that sits last before generation. clean and recovery are guided v2 plus the trimmed working record (448 and 494 words).

**Expect.** Turn-1 parse failures near control (at most 2/64). Task-missing reasoning at most 1. Text-before-JSON turns below control's 22 % if the reminder is taken up. Fewer prompt citations in reasoning. Overflow will probably not move from text alone. If replies over 40k characters persist, cap the teacher's max_tokens at the router (e.g. 16k; it is 32k now) rather than add words. If turn-1 breaks persist, the system role itself is the cause: move the guidance after the task in the first user message.
